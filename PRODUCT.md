# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One person managing their own finances — checking their dashboard daily or weekly, recording transactions as they happen, reviewing budgets at month end. They want a clear picture of their money without the overhead of institutional banking software.

## Product Purpose

Filoos is a personal expense tracker with a beautiful dashboard and balance predictions. It replaces spreadsheets and generic budgeting apps with something fast, local-first, and delightful — turning the chore of expense tracking into a genuinely pleasant daily ritual. Success means the user opens it by habit, not obligation.

## Brand Personality

Warm, approachable, friendly. Numbers that feel like a conversation, not a lecture. A tool that treats the user's money with care but doesn't take itself too seriously.

## Anti-references

- Dense enterprise software (no SAP, no crowded dashboards, no overwhelming data tables)
- Cold institutional banking (no navy-and-gold, no marble/HQ photography, no "we are serious about your money" tone)
- Gamified finance apps (no badges, no streaks, no confetti)

## Design Principles

1. **Warm clarity** — Financial data should feel approachable, not intimidating. Numbers are friends, not verdicts.
2. **Get out of the way** — The tool should be nearly invisible during daily use: fast load, few clicks, instant feedback. The user is here to check their money, not to use the app.
3. **Confidence without complexity** — Powerful features (forecasting, budgets, recurring rules) should feel simple enough that the user discovers them naturally, not through documentation.
4. **Personal, not institutional** — This is the user's own money, not a bank statement. The tone, colors, and rhythm should feel like a well-organized notebook, not a quarterly report.
5. **Delight in the mundane** — Expense tracking is a chore. Small moments of polish (smooth transitions, thoughtful empty states, satisfying interactions) turn obligation into ritual.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Sensible defaults: good contrast (body text ≥4.5:1), readable font sizes, functional without color alone, reduced-motion support. No formal WCAG target — let the design choices speak for themselves.
