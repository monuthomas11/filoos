2. What personal data we collect

Category Examples

3. How we get your data

  • Identity data is obtained from Google when you sign in with Google (OAuth scope: openid email profile).

  • Technical/log data is generated automatically by the server on each request.

Purpose Data Lawful basis

6. How long we keep your data

Data Retention
Your account + financial data Until you delete your account (see §8).

You can request earlier deletion at any time (§8).

7. Security

We protect your data with: HTTPS/TLS for all traffic; signed, HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite session cookies; per-user data isolation (you can only ever see your own data); parameterized database queries; secrets stored as encrypted hosting-provider environment variables (never in the source code); and database backups with point-in-time recovery. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we apply industry-standard safeguards.

8. Your rights (GDPR)

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate data.
  • Erasure — request deletion of your account and all associated data (“right to be forgotten”).
  • Restriction / objection — ask us to limit or stop certain processing.
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format (JSON).
  • Withdraw consent — not applicable to core processing (which relies on contract / legitimate interest, not consent), but you can sign out at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority (e.g., edpb.europa.eu).

To exercise any right, email {{ privacy_email }}. We respond within 30 days.

9. Cookies

The Service uses a single strictly-necessary session cookie to keep you signed in. This cookie is essential to the Service and is not used for tracking or advertising. Because it is strictly necessary, no consent banner is required, but we inform you about it here. The cookie is cleared when you sign out or after 14 days of inactivity. We do not use third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising cookies.

10. Children’s data

The Service is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes (e.g., new processing or new processors) via the app or by email. The “Last updated” date at the top always reflects the current version.
